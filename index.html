Kith

From Old English cȳþþ: not kin by blood, but the neighbours, fellows & friends one comes to know — and keeps close.

I

The mark — an illuminated initial

A blackletter K set as a drop-cap in a ruled roundel, the way a scribe would open a page. Gilt on umber for the app; a rubricated red on parchment, and a sprout-green field, as alternates. The sprig ties it to Loam — your people, in the soil.

Of Kith & Kin

¶Kin is þe family one is born to; kith is þe folk one comes to know — neighbours, fellows & friends. Kith þe app is your bōc of names, holden on your own devices, & shared — when you will it — with none but those you name. A name may bear a Loam key, & so become one you may trust with a shared calendar or a room: þe same person, sworn true, across every app.
